| Time | On screen | Voiceover |

| 0:42–0:54 | Screening | Every application is read the moment it arrives, scored against your must-haves, with the strengths, the gaps, and what to ask in the interview. |
| 0:54–1:01 | Ranked shortlist | So you call the right people first. |
| 1:01–1:09 | Hire | Move them to Hired on one board your whole team can see. |
